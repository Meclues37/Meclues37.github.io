A Summer Through Spain & Portugal

I traveled through Spain and Portugal with my mum during the summer, moving from sunlit streets to old churches, hilltop views, and the sea. The trip felt full of history: stone roads, tiled facades, cathedrals, and coastlines where the light kept changing.

We walked a lot through different cities in Spain, always alert in busy places and careful with thieves, but the beauty kept pulling us forward. What stayed with me most was the opulence and scale of the churches: human architecture and art flourishing into something almost overwhelming.
