Travel Notes

By the Water

Lake Tahoe always feels clean, calm, and refreshing. The blue water and mountain scenery made every stop feel special.

We went on two trails on two different days, and both had stunning views along the way. One memory that still stands out was the second trail. On the way back, the sun was already going down, and we started getting nervous about the light fading. We ended up rushing back in a pretty chaotic and exhausted state, but it became one of the most unforgettable parts of the trip.
