Little Moments With Pengy

Pengy is my blue bicolor Ragdoll cat, born on February 23, 2022. These photos are not really my photography work, but small phone-captured moments from daily life.

She treats every new toy like a life-changing discovery, then quietly retires it after the excitement fades. Her best performances are still the simple ones: stretching herself in two very serious ways and turning every yawn into a tiny masterpiece.
