Travel Notes

A Memory by the Sea

We stayed at Live Aqua in Cancún for nearly a week, embracing the slow rhythm of resort life. Our days were filled with delicious food, quiet moments on the beach, cocktails from time to time, and taking photos for Cici under the warm sun. With the pure light blue sea stretching endlessly ahead, it became a cozy and unforgettable Christmas holiday.
