Above the clouds

Tibet felt completely different from anywhere else we have been. The sky was unbelievably clear, the mountains felt endless, and every road seemed to disappear into silence.

We spent our time slowly enjoying the views, stopping whenever the scenery became unreal, and capturing moments that felt calm, powerful, and unforgettable.
